Wild Loopers are loose in your room. Befriend them by getting them to go through the hole in your glowing string.

How to play
  • Through the hole. Your Looplasso makes a ring of light with a hole in the middle. Swing the ring so a Looper ends up inside the hole. Every trip through fills its friendship ring. Lift the ring off and do it again.
  • Open hearts. A Looper's heart flashes open now and then. Go through while it is open for bonus points and extra time.
  • Touch mode: draw a full loop around it with your finger.
  • Chain hits close together build a combo, up to x5 points.
  • Earn coins for every thread, loop and friend. Spend them on Worlds in the menu. Each new world has new Loopers that need more and more loops.
  • Fill the ring before the clock runs out. It drains if you stop.
  • Dodge the bubbles. A bubble that hits your line snaps it, breaks your combo and costs ring progress.
  • Each mission you finish makes the next Loopers faster.
  • 285 different Loopers live out there. Each mission meets five. Befriend them all to fill your Looperdex.
  • Switch your Looplasso on to play. Switch it off and the game pauses here.

Turn your Looplasso off, then tap Hide my room. Anything else that is the same color (lamps, screens, clothes) gets ignored. It also learns on its own whenever the game sees the lasso is off.

Lasso sound

The game listens to your Looplasso to know when it is on. Off counts as silence, so there is nothing to record for it. Switch your Looplasso on, hold it near the phone's microphone and tap Listen to ON.
