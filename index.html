Looplasso Loopers

Wild Loopers are loose in your room. Befriend them by looping your glowing string around them.

How to play
  • Circle a Looper. Draw a full loop that closes around it. Every loop fills its friendship ring.
  • Fill the ring before the clock runs out. It drains if you stop.
  • Protect your line. If a Looper bumps it or a bubble hits it, the line snaps and the ring loses progress.
  • Keep your glow off the Looper itself. Touching it hurts too.
  • 51 different Loopers live out there. Each mission meets five. Befriend them all to fill your Looperdex.
  • Switch your Looplasso on to play. Switch it off and the game pauses here.

An unofficial fan game, not affiliated with Loop Labs. Best in a dim room.
